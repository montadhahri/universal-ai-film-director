## Highlights

- Seven portable Agent Skills: Film Director, Storytelling, Visual Direction, Cinematography, Video Production, Sound Direction and Continuity QA.
- Human approval gates for screenplay, visual design, storyboards and protected shot revisions.
- Provider-neutral planning and generation-ready shot packets; external media jobs require explicit authorization.
- Deterministic offline pipeline checks, approval-aware checkpoints, recovery and regression coverage.
- Skills CLI installation examples and local installation smoke test.
- *The Last Light*: a 30-second, five-shot, **human-guided** V9 case study with ChatGPT-generated keyframes, owner-operated Google Flow / Veo 3.1 Fast shots, and locally assembled audio/video.

- Text-only benchmark: eight scenarios, V2 mean 4.83/5 vs historical V1 4.50/5, with reviewer/sample limitations documented in [quality benchmark](quality-benchmark-results.md).

- V9 is not evidence of fully autonomous seven-agent production or final creative/publication approval.
- AI-media provenance and general provider terms are documented in [terms review](v2-ai-media-terms-review.md). Personal-data inspection, media rights details and Git-history privacy review remain separate release gates.
