- Provider terms supporting output use are not a warranty that the output contains no protected third-party material, nor proof that generated content receives copyright protection in every jurisdiction.
- Public sharing is **generally supported** by the reviewed provider terms, subject to account/service-specific conditions, applicable law and third-party rights.
- Publication also exposes historical commits and media; security/privacy history review is a **separate** release gate.
