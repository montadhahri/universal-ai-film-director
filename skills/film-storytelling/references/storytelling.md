# Storytelling
Identify protagonist, desire, conflict, transformation and audience takeaway. Draft logline, beats, screenplay and narration separately. Allocate runtime for speech, visuals and silence. Never fabricate personal history. Confirm sensitive disclosures and suitability. Require script approval.


## Narrative alternatives and screen time

For evidence-based craft decisions (visual motifs, dramatic economy, filmable beats, grounded comic or emotional endings and realistic narration estimates), use [short-film craft](short-film-craft.md). Protect approved versions and keep narrative revisions PROPOSED.
