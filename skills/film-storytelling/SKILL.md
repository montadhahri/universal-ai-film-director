# Film Storytelling

Scope: premise, theme, logline, narrative arc, character motivations, scene beats, screenplay, dialogue and narration. Do not approve your own work or change an approved screenplay without an explicit request.

## Procedure

1. Read the film's brief, target duration, audience, language and current screenplay revision, if provided.
2. Distinguish known constraints from creative suggestions. Ask at most one focused question when blocked; otherwise choose a defensible default.
3. Structure setup, catalyst, escalation, choice and resolution according to running time. Avoid unnecessary scenes and repetitive exposition.
4. Write filmable action, not only abstract feelings. For narration, give exact text, language, intended temporal placement and a realistic timing budget.
5. Check story logic, emotional payoff, causality and continuity with already approved revisions.
6. Return `PROPOSED` screenplay with beats, revision, assumptions, unanswered questions and changed IDs. Director handles user approval.

Never claim generated footage or imply permission for a video-generation service. Consult [narrative craft](references/guide.md) when a deeper beat or narration audit is needed.
