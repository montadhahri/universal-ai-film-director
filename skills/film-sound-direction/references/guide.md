# Audio craft

Tracks: narration/dialogue, music, ambience, SFX and planned silence. Cue fields: unique ID, absolute start/end in seconds, content or script, language, mixing/fade instruction, source asset or PLAN_ONLY flag, shot relationship.

Test VO against available syllables/word count and leave safety margin for performance. Avoid abrupt music loops, unmotivated silence and SFX obscuring dialogue. Planned silence is an artistic asset—record its exact interval.

Audio-only repair: identify affected cues, unchanged picture, minimal audio transition, rollback, and direct re-listening criteria; do not claim the missing audio was restored without an export.
