## Repair scenario

**User:** "Narration cuts out at 18 seconds; picture is perfect."

1. Identify expected cue VO-02 at 18s and ask for the actual export/audio if not supplied.
2. Do **not** assume the cause: source file may be truncated, a mute envelope may exist, or a render may have failed.
3. Propose re-record/replace or trim/retime **only the affected cue**; preserve approved shots and music when unaffected.
4. Check cue transitions, narration through 24s, and then full 30s export using real media.
5. Until reinspection exists, say `PROPOSED` or `UNVERIFIED`, not "fixed".
