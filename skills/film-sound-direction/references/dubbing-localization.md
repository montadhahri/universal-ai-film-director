# Dubbing and localization

- Record original text, approved adapted text, language/region/dialect,
  speaker ID, pronunciation notes, and target time range per cue.
- Prefer natural spoken phrasing; avoid assuming Modern Standard Arabic,
  Tunisian Arabic, European Portuguese and Brazilian Portuguese are
  interchangeable.
- Readability and emotional delivery take priority over literal word order.
- Estimate duration with pauses, then verify against an actual audio take.
- Use separate voice assets; do not modify locked picture to accommodate a
  new dub without explicit user authorization.
- Obtain voice actor permission and respect likeness, privacy and rights.
- If lip synchronization is unsupported, state that limitation and plan
  off-screen narration, cutaways or subtitles rather than promising perfect lip sync.
- Review full timeline for truncated lines, repeated phrases, unwanted silence,
  language drift and transition artifacts.
