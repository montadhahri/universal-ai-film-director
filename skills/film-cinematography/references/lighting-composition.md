# Lighting and composition — continuity guide

## Composition

- Identify subject, background and focal priority.
- Use negative space, leading lines and depth deliberately.
- Protect text/title safe areas when relevant to delivery aspect ratio.
- Maintain character scale, screen position and horizon continuity across
  adjacent shots unless a change is motivated.
- For animation, keep approved proportions, silhouette, palette and linework.

## Lighting

- Record motivated source (sun, window, lamp, practical light) and direction.
- Keep time of day, shadow direction, intensity, temperature and contrast
  coherent within the same location/time unless story justifies a change.
- Use key/fill/rim as descriptive planning concepts, not mandatory equipment.
- Avoid conflicting cues (e.g. golden sunset on one side, blue midday sun
  from the opposite side) without intentional story explanation.

## Color and mood

- Follow the approved visual bible's palette and exposure conventions.
- Define deliberate mood shifts as scene-level decisions; do not introduce
  a new look for one shot accidentally.
- Distinguish creative direction from generator capabilities; prompts cannot
  guarantee exact lighting or consistent identity.

## Checklist

Before approval compare adjacent shots for: time of day, light direction,
wardrobe/props, subject position, lens/framing intent, action continuity and
transition. Flag unknowns instead of guessing.
