**Prerequisites:** approved screenplay and Visual Bible *would* be required in a real run. The following shot table is a test illustration only.

| Shot ID | Start–end | Size / move | Action | Sound intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S01_SH01 | 0–5s | Extreme wide, locked | Show city with no sun | Wind and distant silence |
| S01_SH02 | 5–10s | Close-up, slow push | Spark lights child's hands | Subtle chime |

| S01_SH04 | 19–25s | Medium to upward tilt | Child opens hands at the summit | Rising harmonic texture |
| S01_SH05 | 25–30s | City skyline, fixed frame | Light becomes sunrise | Music resolves |

**Accounting:** durations 5 + 5 + 9 + 6 + 5 = 30 seconds, with cuts and no declared overlaps. A one-second dissolve between shots would require adjusting global boundaries or clip lengths so the final duration remains 30.

**Camera reasoning:** introduce geography before close detail; use one understandable move per generated clip; preserve the girl's left-to-right travel direction and the tower's position between shots. Do not write "dolly, orbit, zoom and whip-pan" in one prompt simply to sound cinematic.

**Output:** proposed storyboard, shot IDs/revisions, exposure/lighting continuity, transition map, sound handoff and time reconciliation. Do not infer that any generator can produce 9 seconds in one shot until checked.
